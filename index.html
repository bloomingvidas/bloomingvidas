Our Story

A new standard of care in New Mexico.

Blooming Vidas, Inc. was founded on a simple but uncompromising belief: that individuals with developmental disabilities deserve both deeply compassionate care and the operational excellence that makes that care dependable, day after day.

Born from more than fifteen years of hands-on leadership in New Mexico's DD Waiver system, Blooming Vidas was created to be different — an agency where families never have to choose between warmth and reliability, and where every individual is supported as a whole person.

Founder

Kiley Gifford

With over a decade and a half scaling developmental disabilities services in New Mexico — supporting 150+ individuals and leading complex operations — our founder built Blooming Vidas to combine real operational expertise with the heart that drew her to this work in the first place.

Her vision: an agency that operates with integrity, runs on strong systems, and never loses sight of the people at the center of everything we do.

Our Services

Support tailored
to every life.

All services are provided under the New Mexico Developmental Disabilities Waiver program for eligible individuals.

CIHS

Customized In-Home Supports

Customized In-Home Supports bring one-on-one care into the individual's own home. From daily living assistance and personal care to building routines, independence, and safety, CIHS meets each person where they are — supporting greater confidence and quality of life in a familiar environment.

  • Daily living assistance and personal care
  • Routine and independence building
  • Safety oversight and support
  • Behavior and skill support

Who qualifies: Individuals on the NM DD Waiver with in-home support authorized in their service plan.

CCS

Customized Community Supports

Customized Community Supports help individuals participate meaningfully in community life. Whether building social connections, developing daily-living and self-advocacy skills, or exploring interests and volunteering, CCS is designed entirely around each person's goals and preferences.

  • Skill development and independence building
  • Community participation and social engagement
  • Volunteer and enrichment opportunities
  • Transportation support to activities

Who qualifies: DD Waiver participants with community engagement goals in their ISP.

Respite

Respite Services

Caring for a loved one is rewarding — and exhausting. Respite provides families and primary caregivers with trusted, temporary relief so they can rest, work, or attend to other responsibilities, knowing their loved one is in safe, capable hands.

  • Planned and emergency relief care
  • Trained, dependable staff
  • Continuity of routines and care
  • Prevention of caregiver burnout

Who qualifies: Individuals on the DD Waiver whose families or caregivers need authorized respite.

Family Living

Family Living Services

Family Living offers a nurturing, home-based residential model where an individual lives with a trained provider in a family setting. With clinical and compliance oversight, this high-touch service delivers stability, belonging, and daily support in a true home environment.

  • Home-based, family-model living
  • 24-hour support and supervision as needed
  • Nursing and clinical oversight
  • Individualized daily-living support

Who qualifies: DD Waiver participants whose plan includes family living / residential support.

Nursing

Nursing Services

Our nursing oversight ensures health and safety remain at the center of care. From assessments and medication oversight to health coordination and documentation review, our clinical team helps individuals stay healthy and supports staff in delivering safe care.

  • Nursing assessments and monitoring
  • Medication oversight
  • Health coordination and risk management
  • Documentation and clinical review

Who qualifies: Individuals whose service plan authorizes nursing oversight.

Nutrition

Nutritional Counseling

Good health begins with good nutrition. When an individual chooses nutritional counseling, Blooming Vidas coordinates these services through a trusted contracted provider — making it easy to access individualized guidance that supports healthy eating, dietary needs, and overall wellbeing.

  • Coordinated through a contracted partner
  • Available when chosen by the individual
  • Individualized nutrition guidance
  • Support for specialized dietary needs

Who qualifies: DD Waiver participants who choose nutritional counseling as part of their plan.

Frequently Asked

Questions & Answers

What is the New Mexico DD Waiver?
The Developmental Disabilities (DD) Waiver is a New Mexico Medicaid program that funds home- and community-based services for eligible individuals with developmental disabilities, helping them live in their communities rather than in institutional settings.
Who is eligible for services?
Eligibility is determined by the State of New Mexico. Generally, individuals must be enrolled in the DD Waiver program and have the relevant services authorized in their Individual Service Plan (ISP). We're happy to help you understand the process and connect you with the right resources.
How do I get started with the DD Waiver?
Individuals typically begin by registering with the New Mexico Developmental Disabilities Supports Division (DDSD) and working with a case manager. If you're already enrolled and have a service coordinator, we can coordinate directly with your team.
What areas do you serve?
We serve Las Cruces, Anthony, Chaparral, Sunland Park, and the surrounding communities throughout Doña Ana County, New Mexico.
How much do services cost?
Services are funded through the New Mexico Medicaid DD Waiver based on approved service authorizations. There is typically no direct cost to eligible individuals and families for authorized services.
How quickly can services start?
Timing depends on referral review, eligibility verification, authorizations, and staffing. We move as quickly as compliance allows and keep you informed at every step.
How do I make a referral?
Visit our Referrals page or contact us by phone. Service coordinators, case managers, families, and care teams are all welcome to reach out.
